    Appearance

    Choose Classic (Gmail as Google made it) or Minimal, then fine-tune. Flip a switch and watch the preview.

    Presets

    A preset is a starting point. Every switch below still works on top of it.

    Top bar and layout

    Applied to the Classic preview.

    • Hide Support button
      Remove the ? Support button from the top bar.
    • Hide Try Gemini button
      Remove the Gemini button from the top bar.
    • Hide Upgrade button
      Remove the Upgrade / Google One storage prompt.
    • Hide left app bar
      Remove the main menu (☰) button beside the Gmail logo.
    • Hide right side panel
      Remove the side panel with Calendar, Keep, Tasks and Contacts.

    Privacy by design

    GG runs entirely inside your browser on mail.google.com. It never sends your email or any other data anywhere. The first time you open each Gmail account, GG turns on Gmail's own keyboard shortcuts in Settings → General. Read the privacy policy.

    What GG does with your data

    Nothing leaves your browser.

    • No servers
      GG makes no network requests at all.
    • No analytics, no ads
      Nothing is tracked, logged or sold.
    • One site
      Runs on mail.google.com and nowhere else.
    • Settings only
      Stored in your browser's extension storage.
