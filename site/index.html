A keyboard shortcut extension for Gmail™

GG puts Gmail™ on the keyboard. Point at any email and act on it with one key, learn each shortcut as you hover, and clear away the buttons you never use.

Free forever. Runs only on mail.google.com.

How it works

  • Act on the email under your pointer

    Archive, reply, star, label, snooze or delete without selecting first.

  • Close anything with Escape

    Smart Escape shuts emails and popups, or takes you back to the inbox.

  • Move through the list

    Jump to the top or bottom of the list and page with the arrow keys.

  • See each key as you hover

    Gmail's buttons name their shortcut, so you learn as you click.

  • Write faster

    Schedule send, edit the subject, attach a file, quote a line, paste a link.

  • Answer invites from your keyboard

    Accept, maybe or decline a calendar invite with Shift and Y, M or N.

  • Copy an email in one press

    Copy the open email, or a link that opens it in the right account.

  • Pick your shortcuts

    Switch any shortcut on or off on its own in GG's settings.

Two looks for the Gmail window

Classic keeps Gmail as Google made it. Minimal hides the Support, Gemini and Upgrade buttons, the main menu button and the right side panel.

Each part has its own switch in GG's settings, so you can shape Gmail to fit the way you work.

Questions

What people ask before they install GG.

Read the privacy policy

Does the extension send my email anywhere?

It sends nothing anywhere. It reads an email only when you press Instant copy or Quick quote, and only into your clipboard or reply.

What does the extension cost?

Nothing. GG is free, with no account and no ads.

Which Gmail settings does it change?

Once per account it turns on Gmail's own keyboard shortcuts in Settings, General. Nothing else changes.

Can I turn off single shortcuts in the extension?

Yes. Every shortcut and every part of the Minimal look has its own switch.

Do Gmail's own shortcuts still work?

Yes. GG turns them on for you and adds its own beside them.

Which browsers run the extension?

Chrome and Firefox, and other browsers that install extensions from the Chrome Web Store.
