Private by design

A privacy policy with nothing to collect.

GG runs only on mail.google.com. It never sends your email or any other data anywhere. Your settings sync through your own browser account. The first time you open each Gmail account, GG turns on Gmail's own keyboard shortcuts in Settings → General.
